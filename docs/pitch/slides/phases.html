How we used it

Two phases.

Phase 1 · the grid

Standardization: How do the published techniques compare?

A pre-registered grid of experiments puts six techniques, alone and combined, on one bench, for a single consistent view of the field.

Phase 2 · the research agent

Understand and contribute: Why does the model forget?

The agent explores the bench autonomously, proposing hypotheses about forgetting mechanisms, writing its own techniques, and testing them.
